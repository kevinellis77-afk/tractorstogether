Model rules

Accounting P/L = net guaranteed sale fee minus remaining book value.

Guaranteed fees are included immediately. Add-ons are recorded separately and excluded from P/L until achieved. Known sell-ons/costs reduce the net fee. This is a practical football-finance tracker, not a statutory accounts model.
